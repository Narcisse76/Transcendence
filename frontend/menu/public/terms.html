3. Nickname and conduct

  • Choose a nickname that is not offensive and does not impersonate someone else.
  • No harassment, hate speech or threats, in the chat or in your nickname.
  • No cheating: do not modify the game client, send forged messages to the server, or exploit bugs to gain an advantage. Please report bugs instead.
  • Do not try to disrupt the service (flooding, attacks, automated abuse).

4. Chat and moderation

Chat messages are checked by an automated moderation service before being delivered. Messages judged inappropriate are blocked, and a message can also be refused if the moderation service is unavailable. The number of messages you can send in a short time is limited.

5. Matches and disconnections

Matches are played one against one and are won by the first player to win 3 rounds. If a player disconnects, the opponent waits 20 seconds for them to come back. After that delay the match is abandoned. If only one player is ready for the next round, the round starts automatically after 15 seconds. Match results are not stored and do not give any reward.
