Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Nickname, avatar and language chosen in the menu.
  • A random player identifier, kept in your browser session only.
  • Gameplay data (positions, shots, hits), shared in real time with your opponent. Nothing is stored.
  • Chat messages, checked by an automated moderation service (OpenAI API) before delivery.
  • Technical logs (IP address, browser, requested pages).
  • account data (email, hashed password)

Why

To run matches, keep the chat safe, and operate and secure the service.

Third parties

OpenAI receives chat messages for moderation.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact: trambure@student.42.fr.

Cookies and storage

Only browser session storage needed for the game. No tracking, no advertising.
